# MechLab Design System — Mechanics Debugger

## Product
MechLab is the **mechanics debugger** for Rocket League (Windows desktop). Primary promise: see what you actually did on each rep, why it failed, and what to change next. Not an "AI coach."

**ICP:** Champ–GC mechanical grinders, PC, EAC-off training sessions.

**Preferred language:** measured, inspectable, attempt, evidence, consistency, diagnosis, confidence.  
**Banned early:** revolutionary, AI-powered alone, next-gen, personalized alone, intelligent. Plain speech. No em dashes. No jargon-heavy lab theater.

**One-liner:** MechLab is the mechanics debugger for Rocket League—see what you actually did on each rep, why it failed, and what to change next.

**Above-fold (recommended):**  
"See why the rep failed."  
MechLab measures your Rocket League mechanics attempt-by-attempt—so practice stops being guesswork.  
CTA: Get early access / Download

## Brand
- Wordmark: real MechLab brand assets only (nav wordmark + hero wordmark). Never invent marks, initials, or emoji substitutes.
- Accent: teal `#2fd4c8` / `#7aefe6` on near-black `#05080c`
- Fonts: IBM Plex Sans (display/body) + IBM Plex Mono (labels, CTAs, evidence readouts)
- Atmosphere: dark scientific → evolve toward **forensics / debugger**: evidence panels, attempt timelines, failed vs success contrast, confidence callouts. Keep grid/hairline chrome subtle. No purple SaaS glow.

## Site architecture (§23)
1. Hero — brand + promise + demo autopsy video/placeholder area
2. Felt problem — blind freeplay / can't see the error
3. Product moment — failed vs success evidence
4. How it works — ≤5 steps (Capture → Detect → Evaluate → Coach → Reopen)
5. Mechanics — validated only + confidence language
6. Trust — EAC-off / PC-only / what we don't claim
7. Comparison table — Debugger vs AI coach vs Replay stats
8. Pricing — Free / Pro $12 / Founders
9. FAQ — Bakkes/EAC, PC-only, accuracy, refund
10. Footer CTA

Supporting pages: Pricing (optional dedicated), Compare, FAQ (or on home), Blog/Roadmap restyled, Auth/waitlist aligned to debugger positioning.

## Components
- Nav: wordmark + links + Sign in + Get early access
- Footer: wordmark + utility links + disclaimer
- Buttons: clipped polygon CTAs; primary = solid teal; secondary = ghost outline
- Evidence UI motifs: mono labels, status dots (ok/warn), readout grids, window chrome for product shots

## Motion
- Hero rise-in (~0.7s)
- Subtle specimen blink
- Hover border teal on panels
- Prefer 2–3 intentional motions; respect reduced-motion

## Do not
- Feature-soup hero
- Cards in hero
- Purple/indigo AI-coach look
- Overclaim accuracy; use confidence language
- Redesign the research markdown as a page
